Sprout Isle — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026

Sprout Isle ("the game", "we") is a single-player game for Android and iOS. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play. The short version: your game progress stays on your device; the game shows ads through Google AdMob, which uses your device's advertising identifier.

What we collect

We do not collect anything ourselves. We have no accounts, no servers and no analytics, and we never sell personal information.

  • No accounts, sign-ins, or profiles.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or location.
  • No chat and no user-generated content.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob:

  • Rewarded ads that you choose to watch for an in-game bonus (they are always optional and clearly marked), and
  • occasional full-screen ads between runs, never during play.

To show and measure ads, the AdMob SDK collects data from your device and sends it to Google. This can include:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA, the IDFA only if you allow tracking),
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version),
  • ad interactions (which ads were shown and tapped), and diagnostics such as crash data.

Google uses this data as described in its own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices:

  • Europe, UK and Switzerland: before any ads load, the game shows Google's consent form so you can accept or refuse personalized ads. You can change your choice later by reinstalling the game or clearing its data.
  • iPhone and iPad: the game asks once whether it may track you (App Tracking Transparency). If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", your IDFA is not shared and ads are not personalized with it. You can change this in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your device's Google settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

In-app purchase

The game offers one optional purchase, "Remove ad breaks", which turns off the full-screen ads between runs (rewarded ads stay available because they are your choice). Payment is handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store; we never see your payment details. The game only learns whether the purchase was made, so it can remember it on your device.

Data stored on your device

To remember your progress (island size, resources, upgrades, Nursery bonuses, settings, and how many ads you watched today, to keep ad limits fair), the game saves small files locally on your device using the operating system's standard app storage (SharedPreferences on Android, NSUserDefaults on iOS).

  • This data is not sent to us and we cannot access it.
  • You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the game.

Your device's own backup service (for example Google or iCloud device backup) may include this save data if you have backups turned on. That is controlled by your device settings and the backup provider's privacy policy, not by us.

Children

Sprout Isle is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly allow children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) to use the game. If you believe a child has used the game and you want their ad data removed, contact Google through its privacy tools, or contact us below and we will help.

Third parties

  • Google AdMob (ads), as described above.
  • Google Play / Apple App Store (downloads and the optional purchase), under their own privacy policies.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
